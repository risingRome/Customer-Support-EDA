# Customer Support Ticket Analysis - Key Insights

## Dataset Overview

- Total Records: 8,469 customer support tickets
- Dataset contains customer demographics, ticket information, response times, and satisfaction ratings.
- Missing values found in:
  - Resolution
  - Time to Resolution
  - Customer Satisfaction Rating
  - First Response Time

---

## Key Findings

### 1. Ticket Status Distribution

- Closed, Open, and Pending Customer Response tickets are distributed relatively evenly.
- No significant backlog concentration observed in a single status.

---

### 2. Ticket Priority Analysis

- Critical, High, Medium, and Low priority tickets appear balanced.
- Critical tickets represent a substantial portion of support workload.

---

### 3. Ticket Channel Distribution

- Support requests are received through multiple channels.
- Channel usage is fairly distributed across available communication methods.

---

### 4. Correlation Analysis

Correlation between:

- Customer Age
- First Response Hours
- Resolution Hours
- Customer Satisfaction Rating

Findings:

- Customer Age has almost no relationship with satisfaction or response time.
- Response Hours and Resolution Hours show extremely high positive correlation.
- Customer Satisfaction Rating shows weak correlation with operational metrics.

---

### 5. Priority vs Status Analysis

Observations:

- Medium priority tickets contain the highest number of Pending Customer Response cases.
- Critical tickets are distributed across all statuses fairly evenly.
- No major bottleneck identified in a single priority level.

---

## Business Recommendations

1. Reduce response time for critical tickets.
2. Improve ticket resolution workflows.
3. Track satisfaction ratings more consistently.
4. Investigate missing resolution and satisfaction data.
5. Create a real-time dashboard for support managers.

---

## Conclusion

The customer support operation appears balanced across statuses, priorities, and channels. Response and resolution times are strongly connected, while customer demographics show little impact on support performance. Additional data quality improvements and dashboard monitoring would improve operational visibility.
